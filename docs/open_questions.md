# Open questions and blockers

The following items prevent a verified physical build with unspecified modules or any listener test. A conditional bare-IC wiring guide is in [wiring_guide.md](wiring_guide.md).

The KiCad schematic now selects **bare MAX97220A** and a documented **Same Sky SJ-3523-SMT-TR** jack as a concrete design. The user's actual modules and “Teensy-compatible” jack remain unidentified, so these selections are not a verified mapping to parts already in hand. See [hardware/README.md](../hardware/README.md).

For a breadboard prototype, [breadboard_wiring.md](breadboard_wiring.md) selects three specific documented boards and the amplifier kit's onboard jack. That option still requires matching the delivered board revisions, identifying the SHDN side of JU1 by continuity, and measuring supply and output behavior.

1. **Which exact microphone and DAC breakouts are in hand?** Obtain vendor/board revision and front/back photos or schematics. Manufacturer package pins in [pin_mapping.md](pin_mapping.md) do not specify breakout header order, installed pull-downs/decouplers, PCM5102A SCK grounding, FMT/FLT/XSMT states, or input voltage range. [TDK INMP441](https://product.tdk.com/system/files/dam/doc/product/sw_piezo/mic/mems-mic/data_sheet/inmp441.pdf), [TI PCM5102A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm5102a.pdf)
2. **Which MAX97220 suffix and board?** A/B/E use external gain-setting networks; C/D have fixed +6 dB gain. Confirm pin access, pseudo-differential input network, input coupling, SHDN polarity on the *module*, and supply range before DAC-to-amplifier wiring. Select and verify gain and an absolute listening-level limit with the actual earbuds. [ADI MAX97220 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX97220A-MAX97220E.pdf)
3. **What is the exact “Teensy-compatible” 3.5 mm jack?** Obtain part number and jack/module datasheet; verify tip, ring, sleeve, normally closed switch contacts, and any shared ground or on-board circuitry. This phrase is not a pinout.
4. **What are the encoder and two-button parts?** Confirm switch contact arrangement, detents, pullups, supply, and breakout pin order. Passive contacts fit the assigned 3.3 V GPIO plan; a 5 V module may not. [ST STM32F411RE datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32f411re.pdf)
5. **Can the selected USB source and Nucleo 3.3 V rail support peak load?** Measure/estimate board current, DAC/microphone/amplifier current, headphone output demand, and startup peaks. Confirm JP1/JP5 configuration and whether USB is from an enumerating host or charger. A separate regulated analog rail is a design option only after this budget is known. [ST UM1724 §7.5](https://www.st.com/resource/en/user_manual/dm00105823-stm32-nucleo-32-boards-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
6. **What are the actual sample-clock error and end-to-end latency?** Confirm selected clock source/PLLI2S dividers, 16 kHz WS, 1.024 MHz BCK, microphone data alignment, DAC PLL lock, DMA continuity, filter setting, and total acoustic delay on hardware. The datasheets establish interface compatibility, not measured system behavior. [ST RM0383 §20.4 and Table 27](https://www.st.com/resource/en/reference_manual/dm00119316.pdf), [TI PCM5102A §9.3.5.3](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/pcm5102a.pdf)

The 16 kHz sample rate limits the captured audio band to below 8 kHz by Nyquist. Whether that bandwidth and the eventual acoustic latency meet AURA's hearing-assistance goals needs a requirement and listening evaluation; no medical performance claim follows from this architecture.
